About this experiment

This artwork borrows the strategy of camouflage: blending into surrounding colors. It uses that strategy as a metaphor for the pressure to appear happy and fit in. It does not claim that camouflage in animals has the same meaning as human social behavior.

The loop: your expression → camera → image classifier → creature changes color → you see the response → you decide whether to change your expression.
